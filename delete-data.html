Delete your data — Fair Paws

Android app Fair Paws (com.logicpaws.app) by Plier computers · Updated October 29, 2026

Fair Paws has no accounts and no developer servers. Your game data lives on your device and, only if you turned on cloud save, in your own Google account. You can delete all of it yourself:

1. Progress on your device

  • In the app: Settings → Reset progress, or
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Fair Paws → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the app. This removes all data stored on the device.

2. Cloud save and Google Play Games data

  • Open the Google Play Games app and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data. Then delete the data for Fair Paws only, or your whole Play Games profile.
  • On the web you can also remove the hidden app data in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Fair Paws → Options → Delete hidden app data.

3. Advertising ID

Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google AdMob data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Anything else, by email

To delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, or for any other request, email logicpaws.support@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will respond within 30 days. Purchase records that Google Play must keep for legal and tax reasons are retained under Google's policies.

See also: Privacy Policy.
