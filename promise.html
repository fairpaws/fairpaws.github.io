💗 Our Promise — Fair Paws

Mobile gaming done right. Ethically. · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Fair Paws is made to be a cozy game you can trust. Here is what we promise you:

  • 🚫 We never sell your personal data.
  • 🏠 We don’t keep your data on servers of our own — we don’t have any. Your progress stays on your device, or in your own Google account if you turn on cloud save.
  • 🔎 Ads and purchases do need some data: Google AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID, approximate location and ad interactions to show and measure ads, and Google Play and RevenueCat keep your purchase history. We only turn on what ads and purchases need.
  • ✅ Where the law requires it (EEA, UK and some US states), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • 🧩 Ads never interrupt a puzzle. They only appear at natural breaks.
  • 🎁 Rewarded ads are always your choice. Nothing forces you to watch one.
  • ✨ A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes banner and between-level ads forever. No subscriptions.
  • 🆓 You can finish every puzzle in the main campaign and every Daily Puzzle for free. Extra packs are optional.
  • 🤍 No guilt and no pressure: no nagging to buy, no fake countdowns.
  • 🐾 Paws never gets sick, runs away or dies. The worst is a little grumpy — and a pet fixes that.

Questions? Write to us at logicpaws.support@gmail.com.
