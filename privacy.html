Privacy Policy — Fair Paws

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Fair Paws puzzle game (Android app com.logicpaws.app and web version, "the app") handles and why. "We" means the developer of Fair Paws, Plier computers (Seth Anderson). Contact: logicpaws.support@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Fair Paws is a puzzle game that works offline. No account is required. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not run servers, and no server of ours receives your game data.
  • If you turn on cloud save, a copy of your progress is kept in your own Google account (Google Play Games Saved Games, stored in Google Drive app data). We cannot access it.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You are asked for consent where the law requires it.
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play, and RevenueCat verifies them. We never see your payment card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Our plain-language commitments are in the Fair Paws Player Promise.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (solved levels, stars, best times, hints, hearts, the daily-puzzle streak, the board you are playing, your cat companion's state), your settings (theme, colors, sound, music, language, reminders and so on), the purchases you own, and counters that limit how often ads and offers appear. This data is kept in your device's local app storage (in the web version, browser localStorage). It is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. The daily puzzle is generated on your device from the date. You do not need a network connection to play.

3. Cloud save, leaderboards and Google Play Games (optional)

On Android you can choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services to back up your progress and sync it between devices. When you do:

  • Your save file (game progress and settings, never payment information) is stored through Play Games Saved Games in a hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive. That space belongs to your Google account. The developer has no servers and no access to it.
  • Google provides the app with your Play Games player ID and display name so that the app can show who is signed in. The app keeps them only on your device.
  • Where leaderboards are available, the scores you submit (for example, your fastest daily-puzzle time or the number of levels completed) are shown with your Play Games profile according to your Play Games visibility settings.

Google Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can stop syncing at any time with Settings → Cloud save → Turn off.

4. Notifications

The Android app can show local notifications, such as one gentle daily care reminder from your cat (at most one a day). Details:

  • Opt-in only. Reminders are off by default. They are only scheduled after you turn on Settings → Care reminder and, on Android 13 and newer, allow the app's notification permission when Android asks.
  • Scheduled on your device. The app schedules the reminder itself with Android's local notification system. We do not use a push-notification service (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging) or any server, no push token is created, and no data is sent anywhere to deliver a reminder.
  • How to turn them off. Switch off Settings → Care reminder in the app (this cancels any scheduled reminder), or turn off notifications for Fair Paws in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → Fair Paws → Notifications). Uninstalling the app also removes them.

The web version does not show notifications.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on menus, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. The web version shows only simulated "Test Ad" placeholders and does not load any ad network. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks),
  • diagnostic information related to ads, such as crash and performance data.

Ads may be personalized based on this information if you allow it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).

Consent management. We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, to ask for and store your ad choices. Each time the app starts it checks with UMP whether a consent message applies where you are, and no ads are requested until that check is complete.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: you see a consent message (based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) before any ad is loaded, and can accept, decline or choose purposes and vendors. If you do not consent to personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads as its policies allow.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. Your choice is passed to Google with your ad requests.
  • Elsewhere: no consent message is shown, and Google's normal ad settings apply.
  • Changing your mind: where a privacy-options entry point is required, the app shows a Privacy choices button in Settings that reopens the consent form at any time. Your choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK.
  • If the consent check cannot be completed (for example, when you are offline), the app either shows no ads or requests only non-personalized ads.

Buying Remove Ads permanently turns off banner ads and ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay optional and appear only if you tap to watch one.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Purchases such as Remove Ads, themes, level packs, Unlimited Hints and Heart Packs are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, address or payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your purchase history from Google Play (product, purchase token, transaction time, country and currency), linked to a random app-user identifier that the app creates. This identifier is not linked to your name or email. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts of our own, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not operate servers that collect your data, and we do not use any analytics service of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it beyond the service providers described above (Google AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games Services and RevenueCat).

8. Children

Fair Paws is designed for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us at logicpaws.support@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

9. Deleting your data

  • Data on your device: use Settings → Reset progress, clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Fair Paws → Storage → Clear data), or uninstall the app. On the web, clear the site data for Fair Paws in your browser.
  • Cloud save and Play Games data: open the Google Play Games app and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, where you can delete the data for Fair Paws only. You can also go to your Google Account → Data & privacy. In Google Drive on the web, the hidden app data can be removed under Settings → Manage apps → Fair Paws → Delete hidden app data.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Anything else, including RevenueCat purchase records: email logicpaws.support@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Records that Google Play must keep for your purchases are retained under Google's policies.

10. Data retention and your rights

Game data stays on your device, or in your own Google account if you use cloud save, until you delete it. Google and RevenueCat keep data according to their own policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, delete or correct personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Because we hold no personal data on servers of our own, most requests are handled through your device settings, Privacy choices, or directly with Google or RevenueCat. We will still help with any request sent to logicpaws.support@gmail.com.

11. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security. Connections to Google and RevenueCat are encrypted (HTTPS).

12. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, the "last updated" date at the top will change, and we will announce significant changes in the app.
